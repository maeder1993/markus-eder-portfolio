More about me

I connect laboratory measurements with Python-based evaluation and clear technical reporting, working across cellulose chemistry and biomass-derived materials.

I'm interested in how renewable raw materials become useful materials, and in the analytical work that makes those decisions possible.

My background spans environmental engineering, renewable raw materials and cellulose chemistry. I enjoy hands-on problem-solving: refining a method, connecting scattered analytical files, or turning results into a report that a colleague can use.

Python and human-reviewed AI tools support that work, alongside the chemistry and careful interpretation it depends on. Scouting helped shape my interest in forests and renewable resources, and my appreciation for cooperative, practical work.
